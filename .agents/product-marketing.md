## 1. Product Overview

**One-line:** Bafatá Agri Resources buys raw cashew nuts village by
village in Guinea-Bissau and exports them by the container to
processors in India, the UAE and Vietnam.

**What it does:** The company runs the whole physical chain under one
counterparty — buying at village level, grading, warehousing, truck
haulage to Bissau, container stuffing, export documentation, and vessel
loading at the port of Bissau. Where a buyer does not want to
incorporate locally or take out a new export licence, Bafatá can
organise the export in its own name, subject to contractual and
regulatory validation.

**Category:** Raw cashew nut (RCN) origin supplier / West African
agricultural export house. This is the shelf buyers search on: they
look for an *origin exporter*, not a broker and not a processor.

**Type:** B2B commodity trade, per-campaign contracts.

**Business model:** Purchase and resale of RCN by the container, on
campaign contracts. No public pricing — price follows the campaign
reference price and grade.

## 2. Target Audience

**Companies:** Cashew processing plants and importers in India
(Kollam, Mangalore, Panruti), Vietnam (Binh Phuoc, Long An) and the UAE
(Dubai re-export traders). Secondarily, international soft-commodity
trading houses with a West African sourcing desk.

**Decision-makers:** Procurement and sourcing heads, origin managers,
owner-operators of mid-size processing plants.

**Primary problem:** Sourcing RCN in West Africa carries counterparty
risk. Buyers get burned on outturn disputes, on volumes that were
promised and never appeared, on documents that stall a container at the
port, and on having no idea what is actually sitting in the warehouse
they paid a deposit against.

**Jobs to be done:**
1. Secure campaign volume from a named, reachable counterparty.
2. Get the nuts out of Guinea-Bissau without incorporating there.
3. See what is bought, stored and shipped, without flying in.

**Objections and hesitations:**
- "Who actually are you — do you exist, do you have a warehouse?"
- "Will the outturn match what we agreed?"
- "Do I need a local company or a licence to buy from you?"
- "What happens to my deposit between payment and loading?"
- "Can you clear the paperwork, or will my container sit at Bissau?"

## 3. Voice of Customer

Trade language to mirror in copy, never to translate away:

`RCN` · `KOR` / outturn · nut count · moisture · defectives ·
`FOB Bissau` · `CFR Nhava Sheva` · campaign · reference price ·
stuffing · seal number · phytosanitary certificate · certificate of
origin · bill of lading · jute bags · lot

Buyers write "RCN", not "raw cashew nuts", in their own correspondence.
Copy should carry both — the long form for search, the short form to
sound like the trade.

## 4. Positioning

**For** cashew processors and importers sourcing West African RCN,
**who** need campaign volume from a counterparty they can verify,
**Bafatá Agri Resources** is a Guinea-Bissau origin exporter
**that** runs the chain from village scales to vessel hold under one
name and can export on the buyer's behalf without the buyer
incorporating locally,
**unlike** brokers who resell someone else's lots and disappear when
the outturn is disputed.

**Proof we may use:** the physical chain itself, documented in the
company's own photography — the warehouse, the graded lots, the fleet
at the weighbridge, the stuffing, the crane, the vessel at Bissau.

**Proof we may NOT use:** any tonnage, price, KOR figure,
certification, client name, testimonial, or year count. None of it has
been supplied and none of it may be invented. This is a hard rule.

## 5. Differentiators

1. **One counterparty, whole chain.** Village purchase through vessel
   loading, not a broker handing off to three subcontractors.
2. **Export without a local entity.** Bafatá can ship in its own name,
   subject to contract and regulatory validation.
3. **Stock visibility.** A client portal for stock, lots, contracts and
   shipment milestones — presented as an illustrative preview until the
   real interfaces exist.
4. **Named, photographed operation.** A verifiable warehouse, fleet and
   quay presence in a trade where that is not the norm.

## 6. Campaign Calendar

- **February 2027** — preparation opens. Volume reservation, logistics
  planning, contract and document framing.
- **April 2027** — official Guinea-Bissau campaign opening and
  village-level buying, once the government sets the reference price.

wait for April are negotiating for what is left.

The two informational queries are the AI-search play: they are the
questions buyers actually type, they have thin published answers, and a
clearly structured FAQ answer is what gets extracted and cited.

---

## 9. Tone

Plain, concrete, unexcited. This audience buys from people who sound
like operators, not like marketers. Describe what physically happens.
No superlatives, no exclamation points, no "innovative", "seamless" or
"world-class". Specificity is the entire persuasion strategy.
